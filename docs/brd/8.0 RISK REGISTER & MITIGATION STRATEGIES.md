# 8.0 RISK REGISTER & MITIGATION STRATEGIES

## 8.1 Purpose

This section identifies known risks to the successful delivery and adoption of the Consolidated Client Communications Hub, along with planned mitigation strategies and ownership assignments.

## 8.2 Risk Categories

Risks are classified by:

- Likelihood: High (>50% probability), Medium (20-50%), Low (<20%)
- Impact: High (project failure/major delay), Medium (requires workaround), Low (minor inconvenience)
- Owner: Party responsible for monitoring and mitigating the risk
